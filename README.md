## _Some secrets should remain buried..._ 💀

As rain lashes against the windows of Blackwood Manor, fourteen former classmates gather for what should have been a nostalgic reunion dinner. But as thunder ⚡ shakes the foundation and lightning illuminates the ancestral portraits that line the walls, conversation inevitably turns to the one who is missing—Sarah Williams, whose body was found under mysterious circumstances shortly after graduation fifteen years ago.

You arrived as a guest. You'll need to leave as a detective. 🕵️

When the lights flicker out and the first scream pierces the darkness, it becomes clear: someone among you has waited fifteen long years for revenge. And they won't stop until everyone pays for what happened to Sarah.

## 🎮 The Game

**SILENCE** is a text-based murder mystery game played through the command line. As a detective thrust into this deadly reunion, you must navigate the manor, question suspects, gather clues, and ultimately identify the killer before they eliminate everyone connected to Sarah's death.

But tread carefully. The killer is watching your every move, and time is running out ⏳. Every five turns, the lights will go out again, and more bodies will be discovered.

## ✨ Features

- 🏰 **Atmospheric Storytelling**: Explore the ominous halls of Blackwood Manor, where shadows seem to move when you aren't looking.
- 👥 **Complex Characters**: Fourteen unique suspects, each with their own secrets, lies, and possible motives.
- ⏱️ **Time-Sensitive Investigation**: The killer strikes regularly, eliminating potential witnesses and suspects as the night progresses.
- 🧩 **Intricate Mystery**: Piece together the circumstances of Sarah Williams' death to understand the killer's identity and motivation.
- 🌿 **Multiple Endings**: Your success or failure will determine who survives until morning.

## 💻 Installation

```bash
# Clone this repository
git clone https://github.com/WST-T/Silence.git

# Navigate to the project directory
cd Silence

# Build the game
go build -o Silence ./cmd/silence

# Run the game
./Silence
```

## 🎲 How to Play

Once the game begins, you'll find yourself in the Dining Room of Blackwood Manor. From here, you can:

- `look` - Examine your surroundings 👀
- `go <location>` - Move to another room (e.g., "go Library") 🚶
- `talk <person>` - Speak with someone in the room (e.g., "talk Robert Thompson") 💬
- `accuse <person>` - Accuse someone of being the killer (choose wisely...) ☝️
- `clues` - Review your collected information 🔎
- `locations` - See all accessible locations in the manor 🗺️
- `status` - Check which guests are still alive and where they are 💓
- `help` - Review available commands ❓
- `quit` - Exit the game (but the killing won't stop...) 🚪

## 🤫 The Truth Lies in the Silence

Every character has something to hide. Pay attention to what isn't said as much as what is. Notice who avoids certain topics, who seems overly interested in others, and whose stories don't quite align.

The killer knows you're onto them. Will you identify them before the manor falls completely silent? 🔪
